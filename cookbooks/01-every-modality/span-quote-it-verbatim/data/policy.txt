SAMPLE AUTO POLICY (written for this demo; not a real policy)

1. Coverage. This policy covers the insured vehicle against collision, theft, fire and broken glass while the vehicle is in the United States or Canada.

2. Policy period. Coverage starts on the effective date shown in the schedule and ends at 12:01 a.m. on the expiry date, unless cancelled earlier under clause 8.

3. Deductibles. The deductible for collision is $500. The deductible for theft is $1,000. Glass claims carry no deductible.

4. Exclusions. We do not pay for damage caused by flood, surface water or rising water, by war, by wear and tear, or while the vehicle is used for ride-hire or delivery work.

5. Territory. Claims for loss occurring outside the United States and Canada are not covered unless we agree in writing before travel.

6. Reporting a claim. You must report any loss to us within 30 days of the date it occurred. Theft must also be reported to the police within 48 hours.

7. Personal items. Personal belongings left inside the vehicle are covered up to a maximum of $500 per claim, and only if the loss follows a theft of the vehicle itself.

8. Cancellation. You may cancel at any time by written notice. We may cancel for non-payment by giving 15 days' notice.
